# Air Force Marathon 5K — 2026 course reconstruction

The official 2026 course page (https://www.usafmarathon.com/course-maps/) displays https://www.usafmarathon.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2025-5K-Course-Map.png while its text download link still resolves to a 2023-named PDF. Both show the same course topology; use the image currently published under the 2026 heading as the primary source. Wright State's campus map (https://www.wright.edu/maps) provides additional campus context.

The reconstruction follows Raider Road north, University Boulevard west and southwest, the campus pedestrian spur out and back, the campus path loop, and the curved connector back to the Nutter Center. Road/path centerlines come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0), retrieved November 3, 2026. The versioned source records the selected nodes, ordered path, map hash, URLs, and uncertainty. The organizer schematic determines the course sequence; the base map supplies approximate geographic placement.

The first graph-matched draft took unnecessary parking/access-road detours and measured 6.175 km. Re-reading the map and removing those detours gives 237 vertices and 5.0821 km without rescaling. The retraced pedestrian spur is retained. This is a reasonable geographic approximation of the published 5K schematic, not a certified measurement. Campus turns, centerline choices and the exact start/finish positions remain inferred. No split times are synthesized, and the official 25:54.51 result and 5 km nominal race distance remain unchanged.

Run `python3 scripts/reconstruct-air-force-5k-2026.py` to reproduce the GeoJSON and verify length plausibility, continuity, campus extent, Nutter start/finish vicinity and the retraced spur. The course is registered for overview and lazy close-zoom map loading, also shared with the Aim High Challenge through the existing member relationship. Original personal GPS reconciliation is queued for the next user-supplied annual Strava export.
