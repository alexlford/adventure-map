# Route provenance conventions

Adventure route geometry should make its evidence level explicit.

- `personal-gps`: geometry recorded by Alex through Strava/watch data.
- `historical-course`: organizer, historical, published, or user-authorized proxy geometry used for event context when personal GPS is unavailable.
- Historical proxies must say that they are representative and must not be described as personal GPS or proof that every historical edition used identical turns.
- When user memory identifies a race and an exact-date GPS activity strongly corroborates date, distance, time-of-day, and venue, the record may be marked user-confirmed while keeping organizer-result fields unasserted unless an individual official result is recovered.

The 2006–2009 Snowman Shuffle entries use a shared John A. Logan College course proxy under this policy. The 2025 Colfax 5K uses personal GPS from the matched May 17, 2025 Strava activity.
