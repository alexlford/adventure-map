Pedal mountain biking

Trail systems where the ride is substantially human-powered uphill and downhill. North Table Mountain belongs here.

Lift-served / downhill

Gravity-oriented bike-park days where lifts provide primary uphill access. Trestle belongs here; Strava-recorded ascent is not counted as pedaled climbing.
