Beyond the bib

The days that became stories.

Not every adventure is a race or a summit. This page is for traverses, self-made challenges, multi-sport days, and other efforts worth remembering on their own.

Featured adventures

A deliberately small collection. These have to earn their place.

How this section grows

Routine training stays in Strava. An adventure enters this archive when the route, effort, place, or story makes it distinct.

Rule 01

It has a story

A memorable objective, challenge, traverse, trip, or personal experiment.

Rule 02

It has evidence

Strava, GPS, photos, a race result, a summit log, or another source anchors the memory.

Rule 03

It stays curated

The archive should feel like an almanac, not an activity feed.
