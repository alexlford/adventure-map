A deliberately curated collection. Routine training stays in Strava; these are the efforts, trips, and objectives that have a distinct story.

What earns a place

The archive grows when a route, effort, place, trip, or personal milestone becomes more meaningful than a single activity entry.

Rule 01

It has a story

A memorable objective, challenge, traverse, ski trip, family milestone, or personal experiment.

Rule 02

It has evidence

Strava, Slopes, GPS, photos, race results, summit logs, calendar records, or another source anchors the memory.

Rule 03

It stays curated

The archive should feel like a personal almanac, not an activity feed.
